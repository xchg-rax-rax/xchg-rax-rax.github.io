01 — About

Thinking like an attacker to make systems harder to break.

I'm a Cyber Security Engineer at Netcraft, where I plan and run penetration tests across web applications, Active Directory, Entra ID and AWS. Before moving into offensive security I spent four years as a software engineer building security products at Darktrace and Garrison, writing C++, C, Python and Go.

My independent research focuses on embedded and IoT devices: reverse engineering firmware, finding memory corruption and injection bugs, and building working exploits. It has led to more than a dozen CVEs across Tenda, Vivotek and AVTECH devices. I hold OSCP, CRT, CRTE and CARTP, and a first-class MMath from the University of York.
